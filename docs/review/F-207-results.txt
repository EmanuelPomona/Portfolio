   Which "2.0s":
   - The engine dates the move to the rAF begin time of the frame that dispatches it, and counts
     stillness from there. In that clock, sitting starts 2000.0-2016.7 ms later. At exactly 2.000s
     the strict "idle > 2.0" is decided by float rounding.
   - The event's own timestamp is about 15 ms before that frame began (Chrome's frame-aligned
     input; a browser property, not an engine one). Measured from it, sitting starts 2015-2033 ms
     later. This is the closest measure of when the hand stopped.
   - The "last move" rows use the sitting frame's draw-time performance.now() minus the pointermove
     handler's performance.now(). They differ from the engine clock by sub-ms offsets in where the
     handler and the draw run inside their frames. In uncommitted runs they read as low as
     1999.8 ms; this is a measurement offset, not an early sit.
   - Before 3551528 the frame that saw the move counted its whole dt, so the sit could start up to a
     frame early by the engine's own clock. Handler-time readings were then 1999.0-1999.7 ms
     (uncommitted runs). Fixed, and pinned by the engine test.
